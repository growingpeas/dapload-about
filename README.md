# Trackfold

**Fill a simple MP3 player from your Android phone, no computer: covers that show up, songs in the right order.**

Bought a simple MP3 player and have no computer? Copying songs with a file manager works, but then the player shows no covers, plays the songs of an album in a strange order, or skips some files. Trackfold writes your albums the way these players read them, straight from your phone over a USB-C cable.

## What it does

- Plug the player into your phone with a USB-C cable (or an OTG adapter), choose your music folder or pick songs one by one, tick the albums and tap Copy
- Each album goes into its own folder, its songs written one after the other in play order: simple players play songs in the order they were written to the card, not by name
- Every MP3 gets a fresh ID3v2.3 tag, the version simple players read, and its cover is converted to a small baseline JPEG (500, 320 or 240 pixels), the only kind many players show
- A cover.jpg or folder.jpg beside your songs is added to files that have none
- The music itself is copied untouched, bit for bit; FLAC, WAV and other formats are copied as they are
- Files in formats many players skip (M4A, OGG, Opus, AAC) are marked before you copy
- File names numbered "01 Song.mp3", an old-style ID3v1 tag for very simple players
- Albums already on the player can be rewritten in order or skipped
- An invented sample album is built in, to try it without any music

## Privacy

Your music stays on your phone and your player. Nothing is uploaded, and Trackfold asks for no storage permission: it only sees the folders you choose in the system file picker.

## Good to know

Trackfold copies music files you own (bought downloads, CDs ripped to MP3, your old collection). It cannot copy songs from streaming apps. It writes to players that connect as a USB drive; for a player that connects as a media device (MTP), put its memory card in a USB card reader instead.

Works with simple MP3 players that connect as a USB drive, such as AGPTEK, Innioasis, Ruizu, many SanDisk Clip and older Sony Walkman models, and unbranded players. Not for iPods. Trackfold is not affiliated with any of these brands; the names belong to their owners.
